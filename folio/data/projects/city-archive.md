A stone and concrete building from 1974, known in the neighborhood for the great round opening cut through its courtyard wall. For its fiftieth year the archive asked for pictures of the building alone, for a book and an exhibition. I worked from first light, when the stone turns warm, to the hour when the sky in the circle goes from pale to deep blue.
