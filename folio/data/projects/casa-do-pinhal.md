A family house of brick and pine in the woods above Sintra, built around one long room under a timber roof. The architects asked for the house as it is lived in rather than as it was handed over, so I came back four times in a year: the low winter sun across the brick floor, the green of June in the windows, the lantern lit on an October evening. Nothing was moved or styled; the chairs are where the family leaves them.
