An old colonial house turned into a hotel of twenty-four rooms, with lime-washed walls, terrazzo floors and tall shuttered doors. The brief was to show the place at the hours guests remember: the blue moment before dinner, the dining room set and still empty, the long afternoon in a corner by the garden doors. Shot over three days, mostly with the light that was there.
