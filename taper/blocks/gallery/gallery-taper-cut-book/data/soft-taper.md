A taper keeps more length than a fade: it only shortens toward the neckline and around the ears, so it grows out gently for a month. Mo blends from a #2 at the nape up into scissor length, then point-cuts the top so it moves rather than sits flat.

It's the cut most regulars come back for every three or four weeks.
