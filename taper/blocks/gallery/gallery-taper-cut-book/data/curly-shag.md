Wren cuts curls dry and as they're worn, one at a time, so each one sits where it will after a wash. The shape is built from the crown down: the shortest layers on top for lift, longer through the sides so the curls stack rather than spread.

The bangs are cut last, longer than you'd think, because curls spring up as they dry.
