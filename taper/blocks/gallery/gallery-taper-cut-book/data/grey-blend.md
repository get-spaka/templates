Grey blending doesn't hide the grey: it adds a translucent color a shade darker than the client's own, so the grey softens instead of standing out. It lasts five or six weeks and fades without a line.

Inés times it with the cut, so the color goes on a fresh short back and sides and nothing has to grow out twice.
