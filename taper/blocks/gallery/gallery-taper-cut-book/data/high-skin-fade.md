The fade starts high, just under the parietal ridge, so the shape on top reads round from the front and from the side. Tasha takes the sides to the skin with a foil shaver, then blends up through a half, a one and a two with the clipper's lever, checking it in the mirror from both sides before the lineup.

The lineup follows the natural hairline rather than squaring it off: a sharp edge that grows back without a shadow. The top is shaped with scissors over comb and left with its curl.
