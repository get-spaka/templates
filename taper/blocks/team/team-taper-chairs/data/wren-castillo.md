I trained in Philadelphia and spent my first years being told curly hair was difficult. It isn't. It just needs to be cut the way it's worn: dry, one curl at a time, with you watching the shape come together in the mirror.

I like a first appointment that starts with fifteen minutes of talking. Bring photos, bring the cut you hated, tell me what your hair does in August.

Tasha and I priced the shop by time because we were tired of explaining why a short cut on one head cost more than on another. Now nobody has to ask.
