I learned in my uncle's shop on the west side, sweeping hair at eleven and lining up my cousins at fourteen. Ten years of cutting in other people's chairs taught me what I wanted in my own: no rush, no guessing, and a price I can explain to anyone who sits down.

A fade is a conversation. I'll ask how you wear it on day ten, not just day one, and where your hair grows in a different direction. Then I'll show you the back before you stand up.

When I'm not cutting, I'm teaching. Every level in this shop has stood beside me for a few hundred heads.
