## The evening

At seven the setters strip the Cave, and anyone who wants to can help scrub the holds in the yard. From eight, Rosa and Kenji set the new problems while you watch from the mats, and at nine the wall opens: first ascents get their name on the board.

## Good to know

Pizza from Southside at eight, the Chalkboard open until ten. Members come free; a day pass covers everyone else.
