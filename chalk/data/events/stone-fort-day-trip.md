## The day

We leave the gym at eight in two vans with the pads, spend the day on the easier boulders with two coaches, and are back by five. The coaches show you how to spot, how to place a pad and how to leave no trace.

## Who it's for

Anyone climbing greens or harder indoors who has never climbed outside. Bring lunch, water and warm layers; we bring the rest, the landowner's day fee included.
