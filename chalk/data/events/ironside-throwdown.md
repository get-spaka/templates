## The day

Qualifiers run from ten to three on forty new problems across the gym: climb as many as you like, your best five count. The top six of each category climb the finals on the Comp Wall from six, with the whole gym watching.

## Categories

Recreational, intermediate and open, with youth climbers in each. Prizes from local shops, a T-shirt for everyone, and the Chalkboard's chili all day.
