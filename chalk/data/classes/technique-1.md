## What you'll do

Drills on the Slab and the Vert: silent feet, turning the hips in, straight arms, and resting where you thought there was nowhere to rest. Each session ends on a problem chosen for the group.

## Who it's for

Climbers who are happy on the yellows and starting on the greens. Included in every membership.
