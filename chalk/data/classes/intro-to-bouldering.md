## What you'll do

Three evenings, ninety minutes each, with the same coach and the same five other beginners. The first is about falling and landing, the second about feet, the third about reading a problem before you touch it.

## Who it's for

Anyone who has climbed fewer than five times, from sixteen up. You need no shoes, no strength and no partner: the course includes your day passes and shoes for all three evenings.
