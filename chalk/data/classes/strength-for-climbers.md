## What you'll do

A warm-up, a hangboard protocol, board climbing and twenty minutes of pulling and pushing in the training room. Every four weeks the plan changes, and you keep a card of your numbers.

## Who it's for

Climbers on the blues and above, at least six months in. Members pay $16.
