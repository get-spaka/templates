---
title: "Your rights"
---
You can see, correct, export or delete your data at any time, most of it from **Settings › Your data**. You can also object to how we use it or ask us to limit it. Write to privacy@fernway.app and we answer within a month, usually within a week. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
