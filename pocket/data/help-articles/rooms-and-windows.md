A home in Fernway is made of rooms, and each room can have windows. You only set them up once.

## Adding a window

In a room, tap **Add a window** and choose the way it faces. If you're not sure, stand at the window with the light meter open: it reads the compass for you.

## Measuring the light

Hold your phone where the leaves would be, screen up, and keep still for two seconds. Fernway keeps the reading for that spot and suggests the plants that would be happiest there.

Light changes with the seasons, so we remind you to measure again in spring and in autumn.
