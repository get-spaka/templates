Fernway Plus costs **$3.99 a month** or **$29.99 a year**, after a 14-day free trial.

## Starting the trial

Tap **Try Plus** in **Settings**. You won't be charged until day 15, and we remind you two days before.

## Canceling

- **iPhone:** Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Fernway.
- **Android:** Google Play › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions › Fernway.

When Plus ends, nothing is deleted. Reminders carry on for your first ten plants, and the others wait until you come back.
