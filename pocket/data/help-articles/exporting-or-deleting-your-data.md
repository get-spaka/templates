Your plants, their care history and your photos are yours.

## Exporting

In **Settings › Your data**, tap **Export**. You'll get a link by email to a file with every plant, every tick and every photo, in plain formats (CSV and JPEG).

## Deleting your account

In the same place, tap **Delete my account**. Everything is deleted from our servers within 30 days, and from our backups within 90. If you share a home, the others keep the plants that were theirs.

You can also write to [privacy@fernway.app](mailto:privacy@fernway.app) and we'll do it for you.
