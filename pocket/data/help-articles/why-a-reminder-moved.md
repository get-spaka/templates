Fernway does not water on a fixed calendar. Each reminder is worked out again every night from:

1. **The plant's kind and its pot.** A fern in plastic needs water more often than a snake plant in terracotta.
2. **The season and the weather.** Cloudy, cool weeks slow everything down; a heat wave speeds it up.
3. **Your ticks.** If you often water a day late and the plant is thriving, Fernway takes the hint.

## Setting a fixed rhythm

If you'd rather water on the same day every week, open the plant, tap **Care** and switch on **Keep a fixed rhythm**.
