---
title: "Snoozing or skipping a watering"
topic: "Reminders"
summary: "Swipe a plant on the Today list to snooze it for a day, or skip this time."
updated: 2026-08-14
---
On the **Today** list, swipe a plant to the left.

- **Snooze** moves it to tomorrow, for when the soil is still damp.
- **Skip** leaves this watering out and starts counting again from today.

Fernway notices when you snooze the same plant often, and asks whether it should water less often.

## Quiet hours

Notifications never arrive before 8:00 or after 21:00 unless you change it in **Settings › Notifications**.
