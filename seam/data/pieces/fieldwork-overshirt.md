### Fit

Cut straight and a little long, to wear open over a T-shirt or buttoned up as a shirt. Most people take their usual size; between two, take the smaller.

### Care

Wash cold with like colors and hang it to dry. The linen softens with every wash; press it damp if you like it crisp.

### Who sews it
