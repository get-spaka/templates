Our washed linen comes from a family mill near Panevėžys, which has woven flax since the 1950s. In August, Daniel and Margaux spent four days there.

## The looms

The mill runs twelve looms, the oldest of them older than both of them together. The linen is woven grey, then washed twice in soft water, which is why it never shrinks once it reaches you.

## A new weight

We came back with a heavier cloth, 260 grams to the meter, which will be next summer's trousers.
