Before Ourlet, Margaux spent nine years cutting for the factories of Chabanel Street. Every season, she watched whole runs of coats come back from the stores unsold, and go out again to be shredded.

## One piece at a time

So we cut a piece when someone asks for it. It takes about three weeks, and we know what each of those weeks is for: a week for the cloth to come up from the shelves and be cut, a week to sew it, a week to finish, wash and send it.

## What it costs, and what it saves

Cutting one at a time is slower than cutting a stack of forty, and our prices say so. But nothing we make goes unsold, and we never buy cloth we cannot use.
