Fieldwork began in March with a drawing on the back of a cutting ticket: a jacket with three pockets and a collar that could be turned up against the wind. By May, the toile had been sewn three times in calico, each time a little longer.

## The toiles

The first chore jacket looked like a lab coat. The second was right in the body and wrong in the sleeve. The third is the one you can ask for now, with sleeves cut long on purpose, to be rolled once.

## The shoot

We photographed the eight looks in one morning in October, against the walls between the studio and the rail yards. Two friends wore them; nobody was paid to smile. The contact sheet is on the collection's page, with our picks circled the way our photographer still does it, in grease pencil.

> We shoot everything on the street we walk to work. If the clothes don't look right there, they are not finished.

Every piece of Fieldwork is cut when someone asks for it. Most will still be made next year.
