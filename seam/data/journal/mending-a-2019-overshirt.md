In July, a parcel came back from Halifax: one of the very first overshirts we made, in 2019, with both elbows worn through and a pocket hanging by a corner.

## The darn

Huong darned both elbows in a rust-colored linen thread, weaving over the hole and back, and reset the pocket with new stitches where the old ones had gone.

## Mended for life

We mend what we make, free for the first two years and at cost after that. This one was past its two years; the repair cost twenty-two dollars and the shipping.
