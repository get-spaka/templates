Fieldwork started with the jackets the men wore at the Chabanel cutting rooms when Margaux was an apprentice: boxy, patched at the elbows, washed until they were the color of tea. We kept the pockets and the patience, and cut everything a little longer.

The linen comes from the same mill as always, washed twice. The flannel is from Biella, heavy enough for a Montreal November. Every piece is cut when you ask for it and sewn by one of us, start to finish.
