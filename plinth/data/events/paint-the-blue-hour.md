We start in Room 4, sitting on the floor in front of Winter Night in the Mountains, and count how many blues there are in it. Then we go down to the Studio, where everyone paints their own evening with watercolors, from the last orange at the horizon to the first star.

For children from 6 to 12, each with a grown-up, who comes free. Aprons are provided; the paintings go home with their painters once dry.
