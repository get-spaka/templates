What blue did Eugène Jansson buy for Hornsgatan by Night, and why does it look different from Krøyer's? Mara Lindqvist, a paintings conservator who has worked on Nordic canvases of this period, explains the pigments of the 1890s, from Prussian blue to the new synthetic ultramarine, and what a century of light and varnish has done to them.

She shows photographs taken under raking light and ultraviolet, and brings samples of the pigments themselves. On a Long Thursday: the galleries stay open until eight, and admission is free after five.
