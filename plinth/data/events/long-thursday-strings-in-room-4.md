Every Thursday the museum stays open until eight, and admission is free after five. On this one, the Lindgren Duo plays three short sets in Room 4, under Sohlberg's two nights: Grieg, Sibelius, and the Swedish folk songs that Jansson's Stockholm would have heard.

Sets start at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 and last twenty minutes. The room holds forty, standing; a seat reserved here is held until ten minutes before the first set. The café on the pier serves until 7:30.
