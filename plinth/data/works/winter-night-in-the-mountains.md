The Rondane mountains in winter, at night: a wall of snow peaks, pale blue, under a sky so dark it is almost black, with one star above them. In the foreground, dead trees stand in the snow like figures.

Sohlberg first saw the range on a skiing trip in 1899 and spent the next fifteen years trying to paint what he felt there, in sketches, drawings and smaller versions. This is the final painting, nearly two meters wide. In 1995, Norwegians voted it their national painting. We have hung it alone on its own wall, with the lights low, and placed a bench before it: it is a painting to sit in front of.
