A room in Hammershøi's apartment in Copenhagen, seen through two open doors. At the far end a woman stands with her back to us, very small. The near door is open toward us, its white panel taking most of the light that is left.

Hammershøi painted with a narrow palette, gray, white, a little brown, and with great care for how much light each surface holds. Nothing happens in his rooms, and that is the point: they ask you to slow down until you notice the difference between one white and the next.
