The earliest of Jansson's three paintings here: a winter evening by moonlight, seen from the shore of Södermalm. Snow covers the foreground, where a rowing boat has been drawn up for the season; across the dark water, the lamps of the city lay long yellow reflections, and two church spires rise out of the haze.

It belongs today to the collection that Prince Eugen, himself a painter, gathered at his house at Waldemarsudde. You can already see here the swirling brushwork that later made critics compare him with van Gogh.
