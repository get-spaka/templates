Marie Krøyer stands at the water's edge in a white dress, the family dog beside her. The canvas is more than two meters tall, life size, and it was shown at the Free Exhibition in Copenhagen in 1893.

Krøyer married Marie Triepcke in 1889, and painted her again and again on this beach. Here the moon has just risen, a small disc over the horizon, and its light makes a path on the sea that ends at her feet. The dog looks out to the water; she looks somewhere we cannot see. Stand back from it, as visitors did in 1893, and the figure seems to float on the blue.
