Anna Ancher was the only painter of the Skagen colony who was born there: her parents ran the inn where the others stayed. She painted the rooms she knew, and here it is the blue room of the family's inn, with her daughter Helga crocheting by the window.

The subject is the light. Late sun comes through the window and lands in bright patches on the wall, the floor and the girl's hair, and Ancher paints those patches as shapes in their own right, almost abstract. The blue of the walls is the blue of the beach paintings brought indoors. It is a small picture, and it hangs at the end of this room so that you meet it after Krøyer's two large ones, the way you come in from the beach.
