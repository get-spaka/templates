A veranda after supper, on a summer night. The table is still laid, the chairs pushed back; no one is there. Beyond the railing a garden of flowers drops away to a fjord, and the sky above it is the green-blue of a night that never darkens.

Sohlberg was thirty when he painted it. It closes the exhibition: after twelve evenings, an empty table, and a light that has decided to stay.
