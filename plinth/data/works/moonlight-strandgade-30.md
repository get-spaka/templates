The same apartment at night. The moon throws the shape of the window onto the floor, a pale grid the size of a doormat, and that is the only light in the painting. Everything else is the dark brown of a room whose lamps are not lit.

Hammershøi rarely painted night, and he returned to this canvas over several years. It is the smallest work in the exhibition, at forty-one centimeters, and we have hung it alone on the green wall, at the height of your eyes, so that you have to come close. Visitors tend to stay longer in front of it than in front of anything else in the show.
