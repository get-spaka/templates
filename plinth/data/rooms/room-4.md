Harald Sohlberg moved in 1902 to Røros, a copper-mining town high in the Norwegian mountains, and painted its church and its snow for years. In 1914, after more than ten years of studies, he finished the painting Norwegians voted their national painting in 1995: the Rondane range at night, white under a deep blue sky. We have painted this room the color of that sky, and kept its light low.
