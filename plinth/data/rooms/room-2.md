title: "Rooms losing their light"
number: 2

summary: "In Copenhagen, Hammershøi painted his own apartment emptying of daylight, door after door."
---
From 1898 to 1909 Vilhelm Hammershøi and his wife Ida lived at Strandgade 30, an old merchant's house near the harbor. He painted its rooms more than sixty times, nearly bare, in grays that read as color only when you stand close. The two paintings here are a day apart: an afternoon of open doors, and a window at night, when the moon throws a paler square on the floor. Ida, seen from behind, is the only person you will meet.
