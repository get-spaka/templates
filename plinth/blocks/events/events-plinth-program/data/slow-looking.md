Each Saturday a guide chooses one painting from the exhibition and sits with a small group in front of it for forty minutes. There is little lecturing: mostly questions, silence and looking, until the painting starts to show what it does not show at first.

Stools are provided. The group is limited to twelve, so reserve a seat, and arrive a few minutes early. The painting changes each week; the first Saturday starts with Hammershøi's Moonlight, Strandgade 30, the smallest work in the show.
