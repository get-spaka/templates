Hornsgatan is the long street that climbs across Södermalm, and Jansson lived near it for years. Here it is late at night, empty, and seen from above: a curve of gas lamps running away into the dark, each one a small star with its own halo.

There are no people, no windows lit, only the street and its lights and the hill they follow. The sky and the ground are painted with the same restless blue strokes, so that the city seems to be dissolving into the night. It is the last of the blue paintings: soon after, Jansson turned to the bathhouses and the athletes of his later years.
