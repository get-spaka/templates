title: "The white nights"
number: 5
wall: base
summary: "At midsummer the sun barely sets: a couple on a veranda, and a table left out on the longest night."
---
North of the sixtieth parallel, the June night never grows dark: it pauses. Richard Bergh posed two friends on a veranda above a lake and let the light between them say what they do not. Harald Sohlberg painted a table left after supper and a fjord still bright at midnight. The exhibition ends here, on white walls, where the evening finally refuses to end.
