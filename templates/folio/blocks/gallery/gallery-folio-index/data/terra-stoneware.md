A catalogue of bowls from a small stoneware workshop in Marvila. Each piece is shown twice, from the side and from below, so a buyer can see the glaze and the foot, where the maker's hand shows most. One grey ground, one window, no retouching beyond dust: the glazes had to look the way they look on a table.
