Four apartment buildings from the 1950s on and behind the Avenida de Roma, renovated by their cooperative over two years. I photographed the facades before the scaffolding went up and again once it came down, from the same places, at the same time of day: the pink plaster, the round stair towers, the iron gates and the flower boxes that came back.
