An exhibition of wooden furniture made to be used every day, from a heavy chair of thick boards to a farmer's stool worn smooth by a century of hands and a lamp with a paper shade. Each piece was photographed alone against a white wall, lit from one side by the gallery's window, for the posters, the catalogue and the gallery's site, the lamp in the last of that light.
