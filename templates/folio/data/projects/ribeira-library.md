A public library of board-marked concrete and oak, where a wide stair climbs through every floor and doubles as a reading room. I photographed it in the week before it opened, when the light from the round skylights was the only thing moving, and again a month later with the stair full of readers. These are the quiet pictures; the busy ones went to the council's own report.
