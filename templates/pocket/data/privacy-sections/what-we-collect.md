---
title: "What we collect"
---
- **Your account:** your email address and, if you choose one, a display name.
- **Your plants:** their names, kinds, rooms, photos you add, and each watering or feeding you tick.
- **The plant doctor:** the photos you send it, and its answer.
- **Purchases:** whether you have Plus and until when, as the App Store or Google Play tells us. We never see your card.
- **Crash reports:** what the app was doing when it crashed, with no plant names or photos.
