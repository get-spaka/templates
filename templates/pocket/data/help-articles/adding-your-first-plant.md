Open the **Plants** tab and tap **+**. You can take a photo, and Fernway will suggest what it is, or search by its common or Latin name.

## Tell us where it lives

Choose a room and, if you can, the window it sits near. That is how Fernway knows how much light it gets, and it changes how often it needs water.

## Its pot and when you last watered

- **The pot:** plastic, terracotta or glazed, and roughly how big. Terracotta dries out faster.
- **The last watering:** a guess is fine. Fernway learns from your first few ticks.

Your plant now appears on the **Today** list on the day it needs water. Tick it once it is done.
