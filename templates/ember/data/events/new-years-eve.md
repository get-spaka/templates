Two seatings: an early dinner at six for those with somewhere to be, and a long one at nine that sees the year out at the table.

## The menu

- Oysters, cider mignonette
- Scallop crudo, brown butter and apple
- Lobster from the embers, Savagnin
- Hearth bread and cultured butter
- Hake over the coals, mussel and saffron
- Dry-aged strip, bone marrow
- Burnt honey custard, and something chocolate

## The wines

Julien's pairing goes from the Crémant to a Barolo from 2017, with a glass of Vouvray Brut at midnight for everyone.
