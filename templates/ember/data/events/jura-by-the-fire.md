Julien has poured Domaine Vauclair since the night we opened, and this is the evening he has wanted to give it: six wines from the hills above Arbois, served at one long table down the middle of the room.

## The menu

- Gougères, still warm
- Trout from the hearth, Savagnin butter
- Leeks from the embers, Comté and hazelnut
- Chicken with morels and Vin Jaune cream
- Burnt honey custard

## The wines

Crémant du Jura, Côtes du Jura Chardonnay 2021, Savagnin ‘Sous Voile’ 2018, Poulsard 2022, Vin Jaune 2016 and Vin de Paille 2017.

Seats are at one table of 36; tell us if you are coming with friends and we will seat you together.
