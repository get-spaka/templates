Once a season the growers who send us their oysters come down the coast and shuck them themselves. You eat at the bar or standing, they tell you which tide they came from, and nobody needs a reservation.

## What's poured

Muscadet Sèvre et Maine 2023 by the carafe, the Crémant de Loire and a cider from Bowdoinham.

## The kitchen

The full menu runs as usual; the raw bar is given over to the growers for the night.
