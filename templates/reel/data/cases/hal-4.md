### The brief

Hal 4 wanted skaters to feel it was theirs before it opened, and parents to feel it was safe. Every skate brand already looks the same, so looking like one was not going to work.

### The idea

We spent two nights on the half-built floor with microphones. Each sound became a shape: the crack of a tail, the long hiss of a carve, the clatter of a rail. The wordmark is drawn from the waveform of the first ollie on the new floor.

### What happened

The opening film, shot in one night with the hall's own skaters, reached 1.8 million views. Weekday sessions are up 60%, and at the skaters' request there is not a single logo on the ramps.
