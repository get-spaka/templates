### The brief

Kino Zuid had been closed for two years and the neighborhood had found other things to do. The reopening needed more than a new logo: it needed a reason to come back on a Tuesday.

### The idea

Every screening starts the same way, with a curtain. We made the curtain the brand: a vertical split that parts letters, posters and photographs, and a weekly ident in which the curtain opens on that week's films, cut in the studio every Monday.

### What happened

The façade became a screen, its neon split down the middle and opening at dusk. Membership tripled in eighteen months, and the Monday ident became the thing regulars share before they choose a film.
