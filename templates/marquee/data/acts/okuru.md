Okuru started as a rehearsal band in a Lagos car park and became seven players: two drummers, a horn section of three, a bass and a synth that does the work of a guitar. Their sets run as one long piece, the drummers trading the lead while the horns cut in and out. Under the round roof of The Tank the sound comes back at you from every side, which is exactly how they like it. Their second album, *Overpass*, came out this spring.
