Halcyon Dial write pop songs for people who would rather be dancing. Their live show is four players behind a wall of synths and a lighting rig that turns The Tank into a lantern; their choruses are the kind a whole room learns by the second time round. Saturday's set is the only one in France this summer, with the songs of their third album, *Long Exposure*, and a few older ones played faster than on record.
