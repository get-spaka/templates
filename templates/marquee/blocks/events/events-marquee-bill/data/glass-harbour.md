Glass Harbour make quiet songs very loudly. A singer, two guitars drowned in reverb and a drummer who plays with brushes: in The Tank, where every note comes back twice, they sound like the sea from inside a shell. Their debut, *Breakwater*, was made in a church hall in Glasgow over three winters.
