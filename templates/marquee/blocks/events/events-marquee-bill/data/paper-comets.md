Paper Comets begin each song like a band and end it like a DJ. Three friends from Montréal who met in a choir, they loop their own voices and guitars until the songs come apart into something you can dance to. Their sets in the machine hall are their longest of the year, with the light designer of Hot Static working the beams by hand.
