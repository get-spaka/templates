Amaro Lumen plays long: slow house that drifts in and out of ambient passages, records chosen for the hour rather than the crowd. They have closed festivals in Lisbon and Tangier, and on Sunday they close Hot Static from the roof, as the port's cranes light up and the last swimmers come back up the ladder. The final record is always a surprise.
