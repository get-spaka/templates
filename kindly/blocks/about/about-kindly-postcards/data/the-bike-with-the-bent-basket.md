Callum spent the summer of 2017 renting bikes on Main Street, and Iris spent it planting the island's window boxes. She rented the one bike with a bent basket, brought it back the next morning to complain, and rented it again. This went on for eleven days. On the twelfth he had fixed the basket, and she had to think of another reason to come by.
