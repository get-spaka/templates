The largest room took over the dyers' old drying loft, where the skeins hung from the beams. It still has the beams, and the window high in the end wall that let the air through.

A daybed under the window sleeps a third guest: a child, or a friend who does not mind the sun at seven. The walls are washed in walnut, the brown the dyers drew from green husks, which makes it the calmest room in the house in the afternoon.
