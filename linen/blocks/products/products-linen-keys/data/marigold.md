Marigold is the first room up the stone stairs, and the first to get the sun. Its balcony looks down into the courtyard, over the basins and the fig tree, and by eight it is warm enough for coffee in the hammock.

Its two beds join into a king: tell us when you ask for your dates. The bathroom has a deep tub under a skylight, which we kept when we found that the old roof had a hole there anyway.
