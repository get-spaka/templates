When we bought the house, a fig tree had grown through the roof of what is now room five. We kept the tree, built the room around it, and gave it the terrace the tree had opened.

Everything in Undyed is left the color it came: the linen straight from the loom, the lime plaster, the pine of the doors. From the terrace you see the hills of Monte Albán at sunset, and on a clear morning the mountains of the Sierra Norte.
