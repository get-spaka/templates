From 1932 Don Aurelio Ramos cut suits in this room for half the barrio, and his long cutting table is still here, now a desk. Its two tall shutters open onto Calle del Telar, where the neighbors sweep their doorsteps at seven and the knife grinder's whistle passes around ten.

The walls are a deep indigo up to the height of a hand, as the dyers painted their own rooms, and lime white above. Heavy linen curtains close out the street at night.
