A short one this week, written on the bus with wet socks.

Rain is the best surveyor a walker can have. It finds every dip and every crack, and in ten minutes it draws a map of where the old ground was before anyone leveled it: the puddle that always forms on the same corner, the gutter that runs the wrong way, the stretch of sidewalk that stays dry because it sits on a buried wall.

<aside>That stretch is outside the library. I have checked it in four storms now, and it is always dry.</aside>

Next time it rains, walk your own street and watch where the water goes. It remembers more than the maps do.
