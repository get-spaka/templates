Since July 2020, a Virginia employer can't enter into or enforce a non-compete with a low-wage employee, a threshold based on the average weekly wage that is updated every year.

## For everyone else

Virginia courts have long read non-competes narrowly. A restriction has to be no wider than the employer's legitimate interest, in what work it covers, where, and for how long. A clause that would stop a bookkeeper from working for any company anywhere for three years is unlikely to hold.
