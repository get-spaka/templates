Forming an LLC in Virginia takes a filing with the State Corporation Commission and a fee. That's the easy part.

## 1. The articles of organization

The public record: the company's name, its registered agent and its principal office.

## 2. The operating agreement

The private one, and the one that matters: who owns what, who decides what, how profits are paid out, and what happens when a member wants to leave, dies or stops showing up. Two friends opening a bakery should decide these things while they are still friends.

## 3. The first-year checklist

An EIN, a business bank account, the city's business license, sales tax registration if you sell goods, and the annual registration fee each year on the anniversary of formation.
