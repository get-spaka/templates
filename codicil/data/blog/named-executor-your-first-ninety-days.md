Being named executor is an honor that arrives at the worst possible time. Here is what the first three months usually hold.

## The first weeks

- **Find the original will.** A copy is rarely enough for the court.
- **Order several death certificates**; banks and insurers each want their own.
- **Qualify at the circuit court clerk's office** in the city or county where the person lived. You'll take an oath and may need a bond.

## The next two months

Open an account in the estate's name and pay nothing from your own. Send notice to the heirs and beneficiaries within thirty days of qualifying, and file the affidavit that says you did.

## Keep every receipt

Within four months you'll file an inventory with the Commissioner of Accounts. Everything you paid and received goes into the accounting that follows, so start a folder on the first day.
