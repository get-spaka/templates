I spent my first years in practice at legal aid, with people who had been let go on a Friday and needed to know by Monday whether to sign.

That's still most of what I do: read the agreement, explain it, and say plainly whether it's worth negotiating. Sometimes the best advice I give is to take the offer and get on with your life.

For small employers, I'd much rather write a handbook than defend a claim, and it costs a great deal less.
