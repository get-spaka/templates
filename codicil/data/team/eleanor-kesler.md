I opened this firm with Henry in two rooms on Main Street because I wanted to write the documents I'd want my own parents to have: short, clear, and kept up to date.

Most of my clients come in worried that they've left it too late, or that it will cost a fortune. Usually it hasn't, and it won't. My job is to listen first, then tell you what you actually need, and what you don't.

Outside the office I chair a Saturday wills clinic for first responders and sing, badly, in a church choir on Grace Street.
