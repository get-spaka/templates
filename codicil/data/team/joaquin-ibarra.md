People usually call me in the week after a funeral, or the week a parent can't come home from the hospital. I try to take one worry off the list in the first meeting.

Probate looks frightening from the outside because of its deadlines. Once they're on a calendar, most estates are a series of letters and a lot of patience.

I became a partner in 2021, after five years with the firm. I coach my daughters' soccer team, and I answer emails faster than I should.
