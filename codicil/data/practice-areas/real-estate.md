Church Hill's houses are older than most of the records about them. We read the title back far enough to know, settle what the survey turns up before closing day, and explain every page you sign at the table.

## On closing day

Closings take about an hour in our ground-floor room, or at your agent's office anywhere in the Richmond area. Funds are wired to our trust account; we'll call you to confirm the instructions by phone, because no honest firm changes them by email.
