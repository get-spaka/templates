An estate in Virginia runs on deadlines that no one tells a grieving family about: an inventory within four months of qualifying, an accounting within sixteen. We keep the calendar, write the letters, and tell you what to do with the mail that keeps arriving.

## What we ask for first

A copy of the will if there is one, the death certificate, and a list of what you know about: accounts, the house, the car, debts. Gaps are normal; finding them is part of the work.

Contested estates are billed by the hour against a written estimate, revised before it's reached.
