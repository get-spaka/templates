In Virginia the buyer chooses the settlement agent, and in most Richmond closings that's a law firm like ours. Here is what happens between the contract and the keys.

## Before the day

We examine the title, order the survey if you want one, and send you the closing disclosure at least three business days ahead. Compare it with your loan estimate line by line: that's what the three days are for.

## Wire fraud

We will never change our wiring instructions by email. Before you send money, call us at the number on our website, not one in an email, and confirm them.

## At the table

The deed, the note, the deed of trust, and a dozen affidavits. We explain each one; you sign, and you leave with the keys once the lender has funded, usually the same afternoon.
