We act for employees, and for employers small enough that the owner signs the paychecks, never for both sides of the same dispute.

## Before you sign anything

A severance agreement usually gives you twenty-one days to decide. Use some of them: bring it to a first meeting, and we'll tell you what it gives up, what's negotiable, and whether it's worth negotiating.

Disputes are billed by the hour against a written estimate; some wage claims are taken on a contingency fee, agreed in writing.
