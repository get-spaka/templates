Most families need less than they fear and more than a form online gives them. We start with a conversation about the people you're providing for, the house, any business, and what worries you, and only then suggest documents.

## How a plan usually goes

1. **The first meeting**, 45 minutes, where we list what you have and whom it should reach.
2. **A letter of engagement** with the fixed fee and what it covers.
3. **Drafts in plain English**, sent two weeks later, with a call to go through them.
4. **A signing at the office**, with witnesses and a notary, about an hour.

We keep the originals in our fireproof vault if you'd like, at no charge, and write to you every three years to ask whether anything has changed.
