Ruth Kessler brought her mother's reading lamp to the Repair Café in August. It had not turned on in four years.

Walt sat her down at the bench, took the switch apart and showed her where it had worn through. Then he handed her the screwdriver.

> I didn't know I was allowed to open things. Now I open everything.

That's the whole idea of the Repair Café: you leave with the thing mended, and with the know-how to do it next time.
