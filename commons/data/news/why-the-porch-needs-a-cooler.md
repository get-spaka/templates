Every morning at seven, Marisol meets the truck from Couler Valley Grocers. Every morning, she has to choose.

The two fridges on the porch hold about 400 pounds of food. The grocers and bakeries offer us more than twice that. What doesn't fit goes back on the truck, and most of it goes to the landfill.

> We're not short of food on the North End. We're short of somewhere cold to keep it.

A walk-in cooler in the shed's back room would hold a full week of rescued milk, greens and bread. It costs $36,000 with the wiring, and we have until March 1 to raise it.

## Where we are

304 neighbors have given $22,480. The Long Table supper on February 6 should bring in another $8,000. The rest is up to the rest of us.
