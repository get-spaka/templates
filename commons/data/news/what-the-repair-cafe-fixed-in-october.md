Ninety-four things came through the door on October 10. Seventy-seven went home working.

The tally: eleven lamps, six toasters, four vacuum cleaners, nine pairs of jeans, three bikes, a sewing machine from 1962, a guitar amp and one zipper that took two fixers and forty minutes.

> The sewing machine just needed oil and a new belt. Its owner cried a little. So did I.
