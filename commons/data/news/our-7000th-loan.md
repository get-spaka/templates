On September 28, Gloria from Lincoln Avenue borrowed our post-hole digger to put up a fence for her new dog. It was the Tool Library's 7,000th loan this year.

The most borrowed tools this year: the cordless drill (212 loans), the 16-foot ladder (164), the carpet cleaner (151) and, every spring, the rototiller.

## What we need

Two more cordless drills, a second carpet cleaner and anyone who can sharpen a chainsaw.
