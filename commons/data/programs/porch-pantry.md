The first porch fridge was Sam Adeyemi's old one, plugged in by Dee's garage in the winter of 2018. Today the pantry has two fridges and six shelves on the shed's porch, and volunteers restock them every morning and every evening.

The food comes from Couler Valley Grocers, two other grocers and two bakeries: bread, milk, greens, fruit, eggs and whatever is close to its date but still good.

## The rules on the porch

- Take what you need. Nobody asks why.
- Leave what you can: sealed, labeled and in date.
- No raw meat, nothing home-canned, nothing open.

## Why a cooler

The fridges are full by noon. Each week we turn away about 600 pounds of rescued food because there's nowhere cold to keep it. That's what this winter's appeal is for.
