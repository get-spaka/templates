The lot next to the hardware store sat empty for eleven years. In 2025 the city let us have it for a dollar a year, and 60 volunteers built 24 raised beds in two weekends.

## Wednesday supper

From June to September, a long table goes up between the beds at six. Gardeners bring what's ripe, the bakeries send bread, and everyone eats. Bring a dish if you can, a plate if you can't.
