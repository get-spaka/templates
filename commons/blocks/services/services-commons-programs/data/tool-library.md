Dee Hartwig taught shop at Central for thirty-one years, and her garage was the block's tool shed long before it had a name. Neighbors borrowed her ladder for gutters and her drill for curtain rods, and they brought them back with a jar of jam.

Today the Tool Library fills the old hardware store's front room: 1,400 tools on pegboard walls, each with its own tag and a card that tells you how to use it safely.

## What you can borrow

- Drills, drivers, sanders and saws, corded and cordless
- Ladders up to 24 feet, and a scaffold for porch ceilings
- Garden tools, post-hole diggers and a rototiller
- Plumbing snakes, tile cutters, stud finders and a carpet cleaner

## How we keep them going

Every tool comes back to the bench before it goes out again. Our volunteers sharpen blades, charge batteries and replace cords, and the tools that can't be fixed go to the Repair Café for parts.
