Kids grow out of boots faster than winters end. The Winter Swap turns the shed into a coat shop for four Saturdays: racks of coats by size, boots in rows, and a basket of mittens by the door.

## What we take

Clean coats, jackets, snow pants, boots, hats, scarves and mittens, in any size. We wash what needs it.

## What we need most

Boots for kids from size 11 to 4, and coats for men in XL and up. Those go first every year.
