Twelve fixers, four benches, and a coffee pot that has been mended twice. Once a month the back room fills with lamps, toasters, vacuum cleaners, jeans with broken zippers and bikes with flat tires.

You don't drop things off: you sit down with a fixer and mend it together. Most people leave knowing how to do it next time.

## What we fix

Lamps and small appliances, clothes and bags, bikes, toys, furniture and the odd radio. We don't take microwaves or anything with gas.

## Last year

We mended 1,083 things and kept 4.1 tons out of the landfill. About eight in ten things that come through the door go home working.
