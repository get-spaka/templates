We wrote *Breakwater* in the winter of 2025, in the rehearsal room above the marine supply store, with the heaters on and the ore boats laid up in the harbor for the season. Most nights the lake was louder than we were.

We recorded it in nine days at Pier Nine Sound, the four of us in one room, playing each song until it felt like the night we wrote it. Nora sang every vocal live behind a gobo made of moving blankets. There are no click tracks on this record, and you can hear the radiator on side B if you listen for it.

It is a record about staying: about the people who keep the lights on at the end of the pier, and about deciding, every November, that you will see one more winter through.
