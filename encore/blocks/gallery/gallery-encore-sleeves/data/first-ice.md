We recorded *First Ice* ourselves, in the room where we rehearse, on borrowed microphones and a four-track Marcus found at an estate sale. It came out on cassette, three hundred copies, and sold out at our first shows.

It still sounds like the room: the buzz of the sign downstairs, the harbor horn at the end of *Marine Supply*, and four people learning how to play together.
