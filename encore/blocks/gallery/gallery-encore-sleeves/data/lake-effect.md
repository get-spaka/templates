*Lake Effect* is the sound of two years of playing every bar between Duluth and Thunder Bay. We wrote half of it in the van and the other half in the rehearsal room, and recorded it over three long weekends in the winter of 2022.

It is named after the snow that comes off the lake without warning and buries the highway in an hour. Most of these songs were written waiting for the plow.
