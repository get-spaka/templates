Two nights in December 2024, the Harbor Room full to the back wall, snow coming sideways off the lake. We put eight microphones in the room and one on the crowd, and kept the takes we liked best, mistakes and all.

If you were there: thank you for singing the last chorus of *Halvorsen's Waltz* louder than we did.
