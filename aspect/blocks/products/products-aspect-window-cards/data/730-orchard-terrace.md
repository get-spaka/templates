## The house

A 1931 cottage on a quiet street of others like it, with a shingled front and a brick chimney. It had one owner for forty years.

## Inside

A living room with a coved ceiling, a small dining room, a kitchen with its original cabinets repainted, two bedrooms and a bath. The attic is floored and lit.

## Outside

The garden is the house's pride: espaliered pears along the fence, dahlias, and a greenhouse built from old windows.

## Around the corner

Sunnyland's park and the corner bakery are a few minutes' walk; the bus downtown stops at the end of the street.
