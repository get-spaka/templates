## The house

The end unit of a row of six townhomes built in 2016, two streets back from Fairhaven's brick blocks. It gets windows on three sides, which the middle units don't.

## Inside

The garage and a flexible room on the ground floor; the living room, dining area and kitchen on the second, open from front to back; two bedrooms, each with its own bath, on the third. White oak floors, quartz counters, a gas range, and a laundry by the bedrooms.

## Outside

A roof deck with a gas line for a grill, and a planted strip along the side path that the HOA looks after.

## Around the corner

Village Green, the bookshop, the bakery and the Alaska ferry terminal on foot; the Interurban Trail starts a block away.
