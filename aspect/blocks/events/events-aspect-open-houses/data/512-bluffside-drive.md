## The house

A long, low rambler from 1962 on the Edgemoor bluff, with a wall of glass to the west and the original tongue-and-groove cedar ceilings. The previous owners kept its bones and updated what matters: the roof, the windows and the wiring in 2020.

## Inside

The front door opens onto the main room and the view at once. The living room has a brick fireplace that runs the width of the room; the kitchen is open to it, with a counter facing the water. Three bedrooms down a gallery hall, the main one with its own bath and a door to the terrace.

## Outside

A stone terrace along the back, a lawn to the edge of the bluff, and a fenced garden by the garage for vegetables and the deer.

## Around the corner

The trail down to Clark's Point, and Chuckanut Drive to the south. Fairhaven is five minutes by car.
