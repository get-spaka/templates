## The house

A 1924 Craftsman bungalow two blocks from Elizabeth Park, on a street of others like it. The tapered porch posts and the wide eaves are original; so are the fir floors, refinished last spring, and most of the wavy glass in the front windows. The roof is four years old and the sewer line was replaced in 2022.

## Inside

You come in straight into the living room, with a brick fireplace (now a wood stove) and built-in bookcases either side. The dining room has its original plate rail. The kitchen was redone in 2019 with a gas range, a deep sink under the window and a pantry where the old back porch was. Two bedrooms and a full bath are on the main floor; the third bedroom, with a second bath, is under the eaves upstairs.

## Outside

A flat lot with a vegetable bed, two apple trees and a detached garage off the alley, wired for a workshop. The front garden is low lavender and rosemary.

## Around the corner

Elizabeth Park and its summer concerts at the end of the street, the Columbia shops on Eldridge a short walk away, and downtown ten minutes by bike.
