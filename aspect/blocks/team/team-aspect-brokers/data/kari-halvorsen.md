I grew up in a bungalow on Sunnyland's Orchard Terrace, and the first house I ever sold was three doors down from it. Before we started the agency I spent eight years at a large brokerage in Seattle, where I learned how to sell a house and what I didn't want to do again.

Dana and I started Halvorsen Pike in 2011 with one rule: we walk through a house before we say a word about it. I still write about half of our listings myself. I'll tell you what I think your home is worth, and why, before we talk about anything else.
