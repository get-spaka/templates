Every seller of a house in Washington fills in a disclosure statement, Form 17, and gives it to the buyer before closing. It runs to six pages of yes, no and don't know. Most buyers skim it. Read it slowly.

## Start with the water

The questions on water and drainage tell you most. A yes to "standing water" or a sump pump in the basement isn't a reason to walk away, but it is a question to ask, and something for the inspector to look at.

## Then the work done

Any remodeling, and whether it had permits. In older neighborhoods many houses were improved without them; ask what was done and by whom.

## And the "don't knows"

A "don't know" is allowed, and often honest. Too many of them in one section is worth a conversation.
