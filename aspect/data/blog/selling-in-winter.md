Spring is when most people list, which is exactly why winter can be a good time to sell.

## Fewer homes to compete with

From November to February there are about half as many homes for sale in Bellingham. Buyers who are looking then usually have a reason: a new job, a lease ending, a family growing.

## The photographs

We photograph in daylight, and a clear winter afternoon gives soft, low light that suits older houses. We wait for it.

## What to do first

Clean gutters, a warm house, lamps on in the dark corners, and the porch light on for open houses after four.
