October is usually when the market slows. This year it didn't, quite: fewer homes came on, but the ones that did sold quickly.

## The figures

Across Bellingham, 142 homes were listed in October, down from 178 in September. The median home sold in eight days, and for 100.4% of its asking price. Houses under $700,000 moved fastest; the larger homes on South Hill and in Edgemoor took longer, often three or four weeks.

## What it means if you're selling

Buyers are still here, and there is less for them to choose from. A well-priced home in good order can sell in the first week, even in November. Pricing is what matters most: the homes that sat in October were those listed above the sales around them.

## What it means if you're buying

Expect competition for bungalows and cottages in the first days, and room to negotiate on homes that have been on the market more than three weeks. Ask your lender for an approval before you start looking.
