When you buy a condo or a townhome, you buy a share of a building as well as your own rooms. The papers of the homeowners' association tell you how that building is run, and what it is likely to cost you.

## The resale certificate

It gives the monthly dues, any assessments already voted, and whether the association is in a lawsuit. Read it first.

## The budget and the reserve study

The reserve study says what the building will need in the next twenty or thirty years (a roof, siding, an elevator) and whether the association is saving enough for it. Underfunded reserves mean higher dues or a special assessment later.

## The minutes

The last year of board minutes is where you learn what people are worried about. Search them for "assessment", "leak" and "siding".
