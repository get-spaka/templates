The old town of the south side, built in a rush in the 1890s and kept since. Its brick blocks hold shops and flats above them; the streets climbing behind are wood houses of the same years, with condos and townhomes on the few lots that were empty. The ferry terminal, the bay path and the start of the Interurban Trail are all within a few blocks.
