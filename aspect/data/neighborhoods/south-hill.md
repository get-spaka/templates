A hillside of large houses from Bellingham's first boom: Victorians, foursquares and Craftsman homes on streets steep enough that many have their garden in terraces. Most look west over the rooftops to the bay. Sehome Hill's trails are at the top of the hill, Fairhaven at the bottom.
