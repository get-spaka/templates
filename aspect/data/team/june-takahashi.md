I've lived on the south side for twenty years, and walked most of its streets with a dog. I know which houses on South Hill have the view from the kitchen and which have it only from the attic, and which Fairhaven condos hear the trains.

I also read seller's disclosures for a living, which is less dull than it sounds: they tell you a great deal about a house, if you know where to look.
