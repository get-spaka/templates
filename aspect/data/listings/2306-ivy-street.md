## The house

An 1891 cottage among the oldest houses in the Lettered Streets, with its fishscale shingles in the gable and a porch just wide enough for two chairs.

## Inside

Ten-foot ceilings, a parlor and a dining room in a row, and a kitchen at the back with a door to the garden. Two bedrooms and the bath are upstairs, under the steep roof.

## Outside

A narrow, deep garden with raised beds, a plum tree and a gate to the alley.

## Around the corner

Downtown, the farmers market and the waterfront path are all on foot.
