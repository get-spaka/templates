## The house

A square, four-bedroom foursquare from 1908, set back from the street behind a low stone wall. It has been in one family for thirty-one years, and it shows in the best way: the radiators are all working, the windows have been rebuilt rather than replaced, and the basement is dry.

## Inside

A center hall with a quarter-sawn oak staircase, a living room and a dining room either side, both with pocket doors. The kitchen at the back is plain and bright, with room for a long table. Upstairs, four bedrooms open off the landing, the largest with a view of the bay over the rooftops. The half bath downstairs is new; the main bath kept its tub.

## Outside

Terraced beds down the side of the lot, a gravel path, and a small flat lawn at the back that gets the evening sun.

## Around the corner

Downhill to Fairhaven's shops and the ferry terminal in twelve minutes on foot; uphill to the trails of Sehome Hill Arboretum.
