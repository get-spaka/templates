Dez trained as a printmaker before tattooing, and it shows: everything is built from black, from fine stipple shading to solid fill. He loves symmetry and the slow work of dotwork.

His books are open. Large blackwork panels and cover-ups are booked on weekdays, when the shop is quieter.
