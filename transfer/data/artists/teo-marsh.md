Teo came up in a street shop in Cincinnati, tattooing walk-ins six days a week, and he still loves a Saturday off the wall. He draws a new sheet of flash every month or so; most of what hangs at Lost Wax is his.

He tattoos American traditional the way it was made to be worn: bold outlines, solid black, red, yellow and green, and designs simple enough to read across a room and to hold up for a lifetime.
