Hana works big. Her sleeves and backpieces start at a consultation, where she draws the flow of the piece directly on the body with markers, then on paper, before a single line is tattooed.

She learned in Los Angeles and moved to Louisville in 2020 to be near her sister. Large pieces take many sittings; she books them in a series, months ahead.
