June opened Lost Wax in 2018, after ten years at a jeweler's bench on Bardstown Road. The shop is named after the casting method she learned there: a model carved in wax, buried in plaster, and melted out so the metal can take its place.

She draws botanicals, ornament and fine-line pieces that follow the body's own lines, and she plans every custom piece with the person who will wear it. Her books run on a waitlist; she writes to the next names on it as dates open.
