The winemaker of Seneca Hill Cellars makes wine on the east shore of Seneca Lake, where the deep water keeps the frost off the vines. She brings five wines, the stories behind them, and a bottle or two that has never left the cellar.

## The menu

- Smoked bluefish pâté
- Cured fluke, kohlrabi and dill
- Squash from the embers, brown butter
- Half chicken under a brick
- Cheese from inland farms

## The wines

Pét-nat Cayuga 2024, Gewürztraminer on skins 2023, Dry Riesling 2022, Cabernet Franc 2020, and a late-harvest Riesling she will not name in advance.
