Desert Relay play guitar music from the edge of the Sahara, stretched out and turned up. Two guitars trade the same few notes until they become something else; a bass and a calabash keep it moving. They toured Europe for the first time last year and sold out every night; Sunday afternoon in the machine hall is theirs.
