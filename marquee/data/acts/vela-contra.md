Vela Contra is a duo of two modular cases, one mixing desk and no laptop. Since their first warehouse night in Lyon they have played every set from scratch, patching each track live and letting it run until the room tells them to stop. Their record *Cane* was cut from a single night's recording, and their show is built for big, bare rooms: a slow first hour, a long climb, and a closing stretch the machine hall was made for. Bring water, and earplugs from the bar.
