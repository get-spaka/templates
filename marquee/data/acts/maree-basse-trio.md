Saxophone, double bass and a drum kit with half its pieces missing: Marée Basse play standards slowly enough to hear the boats knock against the quay. They have held a Sunday residency at a harbor bar for six years and play the barge every Friday of the festival, with the hatches open so the sound carries along the basin. Find a place on the deck early, with something cold.
