Les Pétroliers rehearse two streets from La Raffinerie, and you can hear it: their songs are about cranes, late ferries and the wind that comes off the water at six. Three guitars, a drum machine and a singer who rarely looks up. They opened the very first Hot Static from the back of a truck; four years on they have the roof at golden hour. Expect short songs, no encore, and a new single, *Quai 4*.
