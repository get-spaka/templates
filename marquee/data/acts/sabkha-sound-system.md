Sabkha bring their own stack of speakers, built by hand over ten years, and two selectors who play dub, North African records and their own versions of both. It is music for a warm evening on a roof: slow, deep, and loud in the chest rather than the ears. Their monthly nights in Tunis have run since 2016.
