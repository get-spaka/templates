I started by riding along. For three weeks I spent a morning a week in a cab, and the problem was plain: routes were drawn around the warehouse's loading order, not around the hours the pantries were open.

- I asked every partner for their opening hours and their volunteers' best window, and got answers from 131 of 140 in a month.
- We rebuilt the routes around those windows, in our routing software, and gave each partner one fixed weekly slot.
- We loaded trucks in reverse delivery order, so the first stop's pallets came off first.
- Each Monday, the dispatch team and I reviewed every late delivery of the week before, with the driver, until there were too few to review.
