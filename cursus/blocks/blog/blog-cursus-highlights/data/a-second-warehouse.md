I wrote the plan backward from the opening day and shared it with the board, the contractor and our staff, so every one of them could see the same eleven months.

- I chose the site with our Kentucky partners: a leased building near the interstate, with four loading docks and room for cold storage.
- I kept a running budget the board could read every Friday, with every change order on it.
- I hired and trained the Covington crew of nine two months before opening, in our Queensgate warehouse, so they arrived knowing the work.
