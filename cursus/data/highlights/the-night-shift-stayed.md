I asked the people who were leaving why, and their answers were the same: schedules posted on Thursday for the next Monday, a first week spent alone in a cold room, and nobody to ask.

- We posted schedules four weeks ahead and kept each person's nights fixed.
- New pickers had a paid training week alongside a lead, before picking alone.
- Each crew of six had a lead, paid a dollar more an hour, who knew the orders and the people.
