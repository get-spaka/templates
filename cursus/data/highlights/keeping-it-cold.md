We measured first. For six weeks, every pallet of fresh food carried a temperature logger from the dock to the pantry.

- The loggers showed the losses happened in two places: the dock in summer, and the trucks' last stops.
- We built a cold dock with strip curtains and moved fresh food straight from the trucks to the cold rooms.
- We picked fresh food first-expired, first-out, and sent it to the pantries open earliest in the week.
- We fitted temperature logging to all 22 trucks, with an alert to the driver and dispatch.
