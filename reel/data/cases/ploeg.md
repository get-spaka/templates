### The brief

Each grower has a label of their own, and Ploeg would never change it. But bartenders could not explain thirty wineries in a crowded bar, and the importer's own name was nowhere.

### The idea

Leave the front label alone and own the back. Every bottle gets the same bold back label with four words on it, the grape, the place, the feel and the grower, set large enough to be read from the other side of a counter.

### What happened

The wholesale site lets a bar build its list by those four words. Within a year, 35% more bars were listing Ploeg wines, and the back labels started showing up in photos on their own.
