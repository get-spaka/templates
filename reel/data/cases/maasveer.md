### The brief

Maasveer had grown from six boats to forty without ever deciding what it looked like. Every boat carried a different version of the logo, the timetable lived in a PDF, and most riders called it "the yellow taxi" whatever color the hull was that year.

### The idea

A water taxi is a light on the water at night. So the identity is a signal: a flag that blinks in two beats, the rhythm a skipper uses to say "seen you". It runs on the hulls, the quay posts, the app and the films, and a typeface cut from the enamel signs of the old ferry piers carries every word.

### What happened

The fleet was repainted over one winter, a boat a day in a shed on the Lekhaven. The new booking site finds the nearest quay and books a boat in three taps. In its first year Maasveer carried 41% more riders, and people started waving at the boats.
