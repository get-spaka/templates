### The brief

Spaak was selling to people who already knew it. To sell abroad it needed a name people remember after seeing it once, from the side of the road.

### The idea

A wordmark designed to be seen in motion: letters stretched along the tube that compress into the right proportions as the bike passes, like a word painted on a road. The same stretch runs through the website, where each frame size has its own film.

### What happened

The new site opened fourteen countries in a week, with a fitting guide that replaced a twenty-minute phone call. Orders online rose 27% in the first season.
