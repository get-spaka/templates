### The brief

Kade Nights had a great crowd and no face: every stall made its own sign and the market looked different every week. The organizers wanted it to feel like one event without taking anything from the cooks.

### The idea

Every Friday at half past seven the market's lights go on together. We built the brand on that moment: a switch symbol, a countdown that runs on the market's channels all afternoon, and a sign system in which each stall keeps its own name and drawing inside one lit frame.

### What happened

Sixty stall signs were made from one kit in a weekend. The switch-on became the thing people come for, and on an average Friday 22,000 visitors are there to see it.
