Lucía gave the third talk of the spring lecture series at the College of Architecture, on what ten years of rammed earth houses have taught the practice: how long a wall takes to pass the afternoon's heat, what it costs against a framed wall, and why the best walls are built in October.

The slides and the readings she mentioned are available from the studio on request.
