Santa Rita Builders poured the first of the house's eighteen concrete piers this week, on either side of the dry wash that crosses the land. The wash ran twice this monsoon, which was a good test of where we had drawn them.

The steel frame goes up in January, and the straw bale walls in the spring, before the heat. We hope to hand the house over in the summer of 2027.
