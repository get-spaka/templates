The Arivaca Library and Community Room opened on Saturday, with a parade of two horses, a tractor and the volunteer fire truck. Four years after the trust's first meeting, the town has a reading room, a community room and a porch fourteen feet deep where people already wait for the mail.

Its walls were rammed from the soil dug out for its footings, by a crew of eight that included three people from the town.
