The Santa Cruz Riverside Trust chose our entry for a mile of shade along the river path, from eleven entries. It is the simplest thing we have proposed: a ramada every 400 feet, close enough to walk from one shade to the next in July, and mesquites planted between them.

Design starts this summer with walks along the path at dawn and dusk, with the people who use it.
