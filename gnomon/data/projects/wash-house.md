## The site

Five acres outside Tubac, crossed by a dry wash that runs a few days each monsoon and floods a few times a decade. Every other plan for the land filled the wash in.

## The idea

We left the wash alone and lifted the house over it, on piers, so the water and the animals pass beneath. The house is light where the ground is soft, and the mesquites along the wash stay standing.

## How it lives through the day

The porch on the wash side holds the morning, the house turns its solid side to the west, and on monsoon evenings the owners will watch the water run under them.
