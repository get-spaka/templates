## The site

A lot on the main road in Arivaca, between the post office and the mercantile, where people already stood in the shade of a single cottonwood.

## The idea

One deep porch around a room of books. The walls are rammed from the soil dug out for the footings, so the building is the color of the road. Inside, high windows to the north and no glass to the west.

## How it lives through the day

The library opens at nine, when the porch is already in shade. Classes and meetings fill the community room in the evening, its doors open to the east side of the porch.
