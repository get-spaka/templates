## The site

A mile of the path along the dry bed of the Santa Cruz River, used at dawn and at dusk and empty in between, because there is nowhere to stand out of the sun.

## The idea

We won the competition in 2026 with the simplest answer: a ramada every 400 feet, close enough to walk from one shade to the next in July, with mesquites planted between them that will one day join them up. Each has a rammed earth bench and a water fountain.

## How it lives through the day

Runners at dawn, children walking home from school in the afternoon, families at dusk. The ramadas' lights come on as the sun sets.
