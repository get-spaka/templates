## The site

Three rooms of a Sonoran adobe row house in Barrio Viejo, built in the 1880s, covered in cement stucco in the 1960s and cooled by a window unit ever since. The stucco had trapped the damp and the walls were crumbling at their feet.

## The idea

We took the cement off, let the adobe dry for a summer and plastered it again in mud and lime, as it was built. Behind it, one new room in rammed earth and a ramada over the yard, lower than the old roofline so the street sees nothing new.

## How it lives through the day

The tall rooms keep their heat high and their floors cool. The old transoms open at night, the heat leaves through them, and the walls are cool again by morning.
