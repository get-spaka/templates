## The site

A ridge in the Catalina Foothills with the mountains to the north and the city to the south, and almost no shade of its own: creosote, one ironwood and bare granite.

## The idea

Before the house, a roof. A light steel ramada stands four feet over the house's own roof and takes the sun first, so the house below lives in shade all day. The air between the two roofs moves, and the solar panels sit up there too, out of sight from the ridge.

## How it lives through the day

The living rooms face north, to the mountains and to an even light. The bedrooms are in the earth wing to the south, cool by evening. On summer nights the owners sleep on the deck under the ramada.
