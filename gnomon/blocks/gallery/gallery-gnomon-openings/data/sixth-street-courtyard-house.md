## The site

A corner lot in Sam Hughes with a 1930s bungalow too far gone to keep, two old mesquites and the full afternoon sun on its long side. Ana and Joel wanted a house for the two of them and for Joel's mother, who stays through the winter.

## The idea

We turned the house around. Its two wings close the lot's west side with solid earth walls and open everything to a courtyard on the southeast, where the mesquites already stood. The street sees a wall, a deep zaguán and a gate.

## How it lives through the day

Mornings start in the kitchen, in low light off the courtyard. By noon every window is under the ramada or a three-foot overhang. In the afternoon the walls hold the heat back until the evening, when the zaguán pulls the breeze through and dinner moves out under the trees.
