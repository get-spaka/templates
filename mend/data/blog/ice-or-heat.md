It's the question we hear most at the front desk, usually from someone holding a bag of frozen peas. The honest answer: both help, at different times, and neither fixes anything on its own.

## In the first two days

A fresh sprain or strain is often warm and swollen. Cold can ease the pain: ten to fifteen minutes, wrapped in a thin towel, a few times a day. It doesn't speed up healing much, so if cold feels unpleasant, you can skip it.

## After that, and for stiffness

For a stiff neck, an aching back or muscles that feel tight, warmth usually feels better: a warm shower, a heat pack, a hot water bottle. It relaxes the area enough to make moving easier, and moving is what really helps.

## What matters more

- Keep moving gently, within what's comfortable
- Sleep, which does more for healing than any pack
- Come and see us if it isn't getting better after a week, or if it's getting worse
