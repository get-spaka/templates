People often ask me whether osteopathy is like chiropractic, or like massage. It shares something with both, and it's its own thing.

## What I look at

I start with where it hurts, then look at the rest: how you breathe, how your ribs, shoulders and hips move, and how you hold yourself after a long day. Pain in one place is often the result of stiffness somewhere else.

## What I do

Most of the treatment is gentle: stretching, massage, slow movement of the joints, and sometimes a quick, small movement of a joint that has stopped moving well. I always explain it first, and I never do anything you'd rather I didn't.

## What it helps with

Back and neck pain, tension headaches, jaw pain, and the aches of pregnancy are what I see most. If I think you need something else, a physiotherapist, a doctor or a scan, I'll tell you.
