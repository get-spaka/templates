Running after a baby is a wonderful goal, and many people get there sooner than they expect. The pelvic floor and the belly muscles need time and a little training first.

## Before your first run

You should be able to walk for 30 minutes, hop on each leg ten times and do a few single-leg squats without leaking, heaviness or pain. If any of those is hard, that's where to start.

## Signs to slow down

- Leaking during or after a run
- A dragging or heavy feeling in the pelvis
- Pain in the back, hips or pelvis that lingers

None of these means you can't run. It means your body needs a little more preparation, and that's exactly what a pelvic health physiotherapist is for.

## When to see us

At any point, but especially if you'd like a plan from the start. One visit is often enough to know where you stand.
