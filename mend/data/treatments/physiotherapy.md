## What it is

Physiotherapy treats the way you move. We look at what hurts, but also at how the rest of you has been working around it: the hip that took over from a sore knee, the shoulder that tightened while you sat at a new desk. Then we treat with our hands and with exercise, and we explain why each part matters.

## What it helps with

- Back and neck pain, sudden or long-standing
- Sprains, strains and tendon pain
- Stiffness after a fracture or a long rest
- Headaches that start in the neck
- Arthritis in the hips, knees and hands

## What a session is like

Your first visit is an hour, one to one. We talk about what's going on, look at how you move, and start treatment the same day. You leave with three or four exercises, sent by email with short videos, and an honest idea of how many visits it should take. Follow-up visits last 45 minutes.
