## What it is

Osteopathy is a hands-on approach to pain and stiffness. An osteopath uses stretching, massage and gentle movement of the joints to ease tension and help the body move as one piece again. Théo trained in Montreal, where osteopathy is a five-year course of study.

## What it helps with

- Low back pain and sciatica
- Neck and shoulder tension from desk work
- Jaw pain and clicking
- Aches during pregnancy
- Stiffness that keeps coming back

## What a session is like

Most of the session is treatment on a padded table, in loose clothes. Nothing is forced: we tell you what we're about to do, and you can ask us to stop at any time. Many people feel looser straight away and a little tender the next day.
