## What it is

The weeks after surgery shape how well the new knee, hip or shoulder works for years. Owen works from your surgeon's notes and keeps them up to date, with your permission, so everyone follows the same plan.

## What it helps with

- Knee and hip replacements
- ACL and meniscus repairs
- Shoulder repairs and fractures
- Broken wrists and ankles once the cast is off
- Balance and confidence on stairs and uneven ground

## What a session is like

Early sessions are short and gentle: swelling, bending, walking well. Then we add strength and balance, with exercises you can do at home and a clear idea of when you'll drive, climb stairs or garden again.
