## What it is

Pelvic health physiotherapy looks after the muscles that support the bladder, the bowel and the pelvis. Priya sees people of every age and gender, and many come because a friend finally told them that leaking or pain is common, but not something to put up with.

## What it helps with

- Leaking when you cough, laugh or run
- Pelvic and lower back pain in pregnancy
- Recovery after a vaginal birth or a C-section
- Pain with sex, or pelvic pain without a clear cause
- Returning to running or lifting after a baby

## What a session is like

The first visit is mostly conversation and an assessment of how you breathe, move and brace. An internal examination is sometimes useful; it is always explained first, always your choice, and never part of the first visit unless you want it to be.
