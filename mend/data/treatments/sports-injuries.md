## What it is

Sports physiotherapy treats the injury and then rebuilds the strength and confidence to go back. Maren runs the harbor trails most mornings and has coached our return-to-running group since the clinic opened.

## What it helps with

- Ankle sprains and Achilles pain
- Calf and hamstring strains
- Runner's knee and shin splints
- Shoulder pain from swimming, climbing or throwing
- Getting back to sport after a long break

## What a session is like

We look at how you move in your sport, not just on a table: a short run on the treadmill, a few hops, a squat. Your plan has clear steps back to full training, so you know when it's safe to do more.
