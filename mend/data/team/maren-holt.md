I became a physiotherapist after a knee injury in my twenties, when the most useful thing anyone did for me was explain what was going on. That's still the part of the job I care about most.

I see a lot of runners, and a lot of people whose backs have had enough of their chairs. Whatever brings you in, we'll find what you can do this week, not just what you should stop doing.

Théo and I opened Tern in 2018 because we wanted a clinic where an hour meant an hour. Outside the clinic I run the harbor trails most mornings, slowly.
