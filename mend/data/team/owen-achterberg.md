I spent my first years as a physiotherapist on a hospital ward, helping people take their first steps after surgery. I still love that work, and I like seeing it through all the way to the garden, the golf course or the stairs at home.

I also see many people with pain that has outlasted the injury. It's real, it's common, and it improves: we work on it with movement, patience and a clear explanation of what's happening.
