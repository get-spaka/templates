Many people wait years before they talk to anyone about leaking, pain or pressure. I'd like to make that first conversation easy, and the rest of the work practical.

My room is private and quiet, and the first visit is mostly talking. Nothing is examined without your say, and we go at whatever pace suits you.

I joined Tern in 2020 and run our small classes for new parents returning to exercise.
