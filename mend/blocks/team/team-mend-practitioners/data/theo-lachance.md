I trained in Montreal, where I learned to treat the person on the table rather than the spot they point to. A sore neck often has a lot to do with the shoulders, the breathing and the week someone has had.

My sessions are mostly hands-on and quiet. I'll tell you what I'm doing as I go, and you can always ask me to stop or to work more gently.

Je parle français: you're welcome to have your whole visit in French.
