Nightjar began as a script that one of us wrote after being woken at 3 am, for the fourth time in a week, by a check that had failed once and then been fine.

Today forty teams from the waitlist start using the first real version. It watches websites and APIs from one region, every minute, and sends one email or one push when something is down for more than two checks in a row. That is all, for now.

We will publish what we ship here every two weeks. Write to us at hello@nightjar.dev; all five of us read it.
