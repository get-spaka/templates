Four months after the private alpha, Nightjar opens to everyone on the waitlist. We let in about two hundred teams a week, in the order you joined, so that we can answer every message ourselves.

What is new since the alpha: checks from twelve regions on three continents; incidents that group related failures; on-call rotations with quiet hours; and SMS and phone calls for what cannot wait until morning.

The beta is free. Everyone who joined the waitlist before 1.0 gets the Team plan at half price for its first year.

## Fixed

- Certificates that expire in less than a day are now reported as expiring, not as already expired.
- Invitations sent to an address with capital letters can now be accepted.
