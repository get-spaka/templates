Kate and Ruth's barn is ten miles from Hollow Run, so their flowers traveled less than their guests. Twelve pails of the season's last dahlias, chrysanthemums, zinnias and ninebark gone red, picked up on the Friday in their own truck.

Ruth's sister arranged the long table; Kate made her own bouquet the morning of, from the pail we'd marked for her.
