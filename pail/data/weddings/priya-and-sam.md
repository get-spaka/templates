Priya and Sam came to a consultation with a list of every friend who had ever said they liked arranging flowers. We sent them home with nine pails on the Friday: peonies in bud, sweet peas, the first garden roses and ninebark for the green.

Nia's sheet said which stems to cut first and which to leave in the cooler of their garage overnight. By the Saturday every jar on every table had been made by someone at the wedding.
