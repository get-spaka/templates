Ana wanted the flowers to look as though they had grown under the same glass. We built the tables around Hollow Run's cafe au lait dahlias, with lisianthus and cosmos loose between them, and hung eucalyptus along the arch where they married.

The conservatory's own plants did half the work, so we kept everything low and loose: bud vases and old glass, a dozen to a table, nothing above eye level.

## On the day

We were in at seven, set up by noon and back at eleven at night to clear it all away. Ana's mother took home the arch's eucalyptus to dry.
