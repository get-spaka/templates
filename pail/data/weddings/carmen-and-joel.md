A May wedding is a wedding of ranunculus. Carmen wanted coral and white, so we lined the aisle with low boxes of ranunculus, anemones and tulips, and made a bouquet she could carry for an hour without tiring.

After the ceremony the aisle boxes went straight onto the reception's tables: one set of flowers, used twice, which kept the budget where they wanted it.
