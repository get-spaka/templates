  **TEDDY:** So where does it actually come from?

  **LORRAINE:** Scots-Irish settlers in the 1700s. "You ones." Say it fast enough, enough times, and you get yinz. English had dropped "thou" and needed a way to tell one person from a room full of them. Every region found its own fix.

Teddy grew up saying *yinz*, stopped in college, and started again the day he moved home. This week Marisol and our guest, dialectologist **Dr. Lorraine Hatch**, explain why English needed a plural *you* in the first place, and how Pittsburgh, the South and the Midwest each built one out of spare parts.
