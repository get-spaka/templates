Our producer Hana keeps a tally of every *um* she cuts. Speech scientist **Dr. Kofi Mensah** asks her to stop: in his lab, listeners remember a word better when it comes right after an *um*. We talk about *uh* versus *um*, why some languages hum instead, and why a speech with none sounds faintly robotic.
