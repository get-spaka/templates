## The house as we found it

A front room with picture rails, a long blank wall and two bookcases from a big-box store bowing under their shelves, with the rest of the books in boxes.

## What we made

Bookcases on the whole long wall, floor to ceiling, their shelves set to the books they hold, with a rolling ladder on a brass rail. The cases were made in six sections at the bench, with maple shelves that will not sag, and scribed to a wall that leans an inch over its height.

## The finish

Primer and two coats of paint sprayed at the workshop, the last coat brushed on site once the cases were fitted, and the ladder painted to match.
