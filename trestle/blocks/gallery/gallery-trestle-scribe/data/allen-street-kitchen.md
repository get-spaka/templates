## The house as we found it

The kitchen had been redone in the 1970s: flat laminate cabinets, a vinyl floor worn through at the sink and a stud wall where the pantry used to be. Behind the plaster we found the pantry's old shelf cleats and the outline of its door.

## What we made

A run of painted poplar cabinets with inset doors, a farmhouse sink under the back window, drawers in white oak with hand-cut dovetails, and a tall pantry cupboard standing where the old pantry was. Everything was made at our benches on Union Street and fitted in four weeks.

## The finish

Two coats of milk paint on the poplar, hardwax oil on the oak and unlacquered brass hinges and knobs that will darken with use. The ceiling went back to its full nine feet.
