## The house as we found it

Fourteen two-over-two sashes, painted shut, their cords cut and their putty falling out, and under them clapboards shedding their paint in sheets. Two window companies had quoted for vinyl.

## What we made

The sashes came to the workshop while the openings were boarded. We stripped them, splice-repaired the rotten bottom rails with old-growth pine, reglazed them in their own wavy glass, and fitted new parting beads and weatherstrip. On the house, the clapboards were scraped back to sound paint and the split ones replaced.

## The finish

Linseed putty, linseed paint in the house's slate blue, and new cotton cords over the original weights; the clapboards primed and painted in their cream. Each sash now slides with one finger.
