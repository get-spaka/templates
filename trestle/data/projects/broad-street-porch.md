## The house as we found it

The porch floor had worn through its paint to the bare boards, a dozen of them soft along the outer edge where the rain reaches, and the square posts had rotted at their feet where they meet the deck. The floor fell an inch toward the street.

## What we made

We lifted the soft boards, sistered three joists and leveled the frame, then laid new tongue-and-groove boards in black locust, cut to the old widths and run in the old pattern around the corner. The post bases were cut out and spliced with Douglas fir, and the skirt board along the edge replaced.

## The finish

Every new board primed on all four faces before it went down, then two coats of porch gray on the floor and the house's own cream on the posts and trim.
