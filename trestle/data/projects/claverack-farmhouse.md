## The house as we found it

Stone walls two feet thick, sound, and almost everything inside them tired: plywood over the pine floors, the old kitchen's hearth closed behind a stud wall, its plaster falling and its ceiling held up on a prop, and a stair so steep the owners used the back one.

## What we made

Every floorboard was numbered, lifted, planed and relaid on leveled joists. We opened the great hearth again, framed a new ceiling under the old beams, and made board doors and small-paned casements to match the few originals left, working with our plumber and electrician one room at a time.

## The finish

Lime plaster on the stone, linseed oil on the pine, and milk paint on the doors in three colors found under the old layers.
