## The house as we found it

A stair with good oak treads and a rail that moved when you leaned on it: a turned newel screwed to the bottom step and a run of thin painted spindles, several of them loose.

## What we made

A boxed newel in white oak, built up around a post bolted through to the framing, a handrail shaped by hand from one length of oak, and square iron balusters set into the treads, two to a step. Drawn full size on the workshop floor, made at the bench and fitted in two days.

## The finish

Hardwax oil on the newel and the rail, matched to the old treads; the balusters left in their black.
